Dunker is a fat-bubble display face with rounded counters and a heavy, even weight.

Each letter is built from full pillowed forms that pad into one another, with little flecks of negative space where the strokes meet. Nothing tapers — the weight stays the same from one corner to the next — and the bowls of the b, d, p, and q expand until they almost touch the next letter. There is a swing to the lowercase that comes straight out of the throw-up tradition, and a warmth in the caps that feels closer to a 70s record sleeve.

You can picture it sprayed five colours deep across a cinderblock wall behind a half-court, or stitched in chenille across the chest of a varsity hoodie.

The font is built with advanced OpenType functionality and guaranteed top-notch quality, containing stylistic and contextual alternates, ligatures and more automatic and manual features; all to give you full control and customizability.
It has extensive lingual support, covering all Latin-based languages, from North Europa to South Africa, from America to South-East Asia. It contains all characters and symbols you'll ever need, including all punctuation and numbers.
