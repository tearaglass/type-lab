# Research pass 01 — 2026-08-21

49 candidates were selected from eight supplied sources. The pass favors disagreement: book serifs, signage families, network monos, non-Latin systems, bitmap and equipment-display references, mutable variable fonts, and highly specific title faces. Five OFL WOFF2 files were retained for immediate testing; all other candidates remain source-linked references.

## Strongest candidates

- **Editorial / literary:** Messer is the most credible long-form purchase candidate because its sharpened Weiss Antiqua structure stays distinctive without becoming display-only. TXT25 / NaN Tragedy introduces visible imbalance at text sizes. Mara des Bois and Avara are stronger for covers and sectional hierarchy.
- **UI / body:** MD IO is the best technical reading candidate, with strong small-size differentiation and a serious italic. Hasköy is the practical wide-range control. Biblio is valuable wherever narrow institutional text and navigation overlap. Karrik is the deliberately rougher interface option.
- **Technical / system:** The Future Mono offers an unusually calm historical counterfactual; Compagnon makes hierarchy from conflicting typewriter periods; Whois suits network records; Minisystem gives music and equipment interfaces an authentic segmented-display source.
- **Experimental:** Music Box has the clearest performance use because frequency and amplitude can map directly to audio. Mini Mochi exposes three visually distinct axes. Fungal treats growth as a collaborative variable system and supports multiple scripts. Motorix is worth purchasing for its built-in randomized behavior.
- **Archival / institutional:** Biblio began as actual library signage. Compagnon feels like a working documentary system rather than a costume. Degheest is an operable archive containing several incompatible families. Betsy Flanagan comes from keyboard diagrams in software manuals.
- **Early digital / internet:** VG5000 and Karma have specific computer lineages rather than generic pixel nostalgia. Fake Receipt captures transactional ephemera. 1980 Portable, TRS-Million, and Minisystem cover bitmap, segmented clock, and consumer-audio display cultures.
- **Display / atmospheric:** Digestive is the strongest organic title face; its tension comes from anatomy rather than effects. Cedar makes digital geometry feel carved. Dunkel Sans brings Hangul and Korean poster lettering into the collection. CirrusCumulus creates softness entirely through silhouette.
- **Strangest wildcards:** Goosebreak introduces Canadian Aboriginal Syllabics and a non-Latin design problem. BianZhiDai combines ASCII shading with woven-plastic-bag construction. PowerPack makes variable axes behave like device state. Gooper Deck tests the practical limit of swash and fat-face excess.

## Variable and OpenType behavior

- Music Box: `FREQ` −100–100 and `AMPL` 0–100.
- Mini Mochi: `STCH`, `FLAV`, and `SLCE`, each 0–100.
- Caffeine: independent `SCRI` and `SCRA` rough-construction axes.
- PowerPack: `CHRG` and `POWR`, useful for visible interface state.
- Fungal: `GROW` and `THCK`, plus unusually broad script support.
- Péix advertises four axes and 10+ styles, but its actual axis records remain intentionally blank until its web license is clarified and a current binary can be inspected.

## Licensing problems

- En Travaux’s 2025 EULA broadly mentions web and app use but also prohibits Internet font serving without prior consent. All four entries remain reference-only pending written clarification.
- Limkin is described as open source, but the inspected download archive contained no license file. It was not retained.
- BianZhiDai’s upstream license changed on 2026-02-26. Current releases require designer and publisher credit; older OFL downloads retain their original terms.
- Future Fonts entries use foundry-specific paid terms. Point Type’s exact checkout terms were not publicly surfaced. PSY/OPS requires the license appropriate to desktop, web, or application use. No binaries from these sources were bundled.
- UNCUT is an excellent discovery catalogue, but it asks users to resolve license questions with the original creators. Those records remain references until each upstream package is inspected.

## Purchase and deprioritization

Worth purchasing or trialing first: **MD IO** for code and dense data, **Messer** for literary text, **Biblio** for compact institutional systems, **Motorix** for generative behavior, and **Digestive** for a singular display voice.

Deprioritize without deleting: **DGTL25** and **Chicagoland** until Point Type’s specimens and license terms can be reviewed more deeply; **Péridia** until it proves more useful than Messer or Mara des Bois; **Airbag** until its difference from libre Pilowlava is visible enough to justify its license friction.

## Systems worth testing

- **Digestive + Hasköy + MD IO:** organic display, quiet UI/body, precise technical mono.
- **Mara des Bois + Karrik + Whois:** inscriptional serif, irregular interface sans, network mono.
- **Messer + Biblio + The Future Mono:** literary reading, compact institutional navigation, restrained machine data.
- **Fungal + Minipax + Compagnon:** mutable display, quiet editorial serif, documentary mono.

No final family is selected. The next pass should visually compare these systems, inspect upstream packages for the strongest open references, and purchase only after project-specific specimen testing.
