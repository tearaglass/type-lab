# Research pass 02 — 2026-08-21

40 candidates were added from four supplied directories: typolibres, The Punch, TypeTofu, and Typedex’s Indie Fonts collection. Each record preserves the discovery directory separately from the official foundry source. Nineteen OFL WOFF2 packages from Tunera were inspected and retained beside their license files, bringing the lab to 89 candidates and 24 actual local specimens.

## Strongest candidates

- **Editorial / literary:** Nyght Serif is the immediate libre workhorse: five weights, italics, and Cyrillic make it suitable for real reading systems. Eliza is the strongest paid hybrid, interrupting a transitional serif with square digital structures. Luperca and Picaflor are useful for studying optical-size behavior rather than merely picking one display cut.
- **UI / body:** Chairman has the most promising system architecture because Text, Display, Mono, and Semi-Mono deliberately share a voice. Panos adds serious Greek/Latin body coverage. Lelo Mono is unusually complete for a technical family, with six weights and italics.
- **Technical / system:** GT Mechanik is the standout paid system: Mono, Semi, and Poly constructions translate electromechanical constraints across roles. Tanklager is the strongest locally testable compact label face. Sector Collection pushes a single segmented grid into a 700-glyph professional family.
- **Experimental:** Exposure has the clearest conceptual variable axis, modeling under- and overexposed phototype. Bertin turns information-design theory into modular variable typography. Teranoptia behaves halfway between alphabet, pictogram, and figure. Ampoule is an unusually broad libre construction kit.
- **Archival / institutional:** Roubaix Industrielle is the best open candidate because its optical sizes and trap intensities come from production logic. ABC Schengen uses contemporary logistics and manufacturing language without fake bureaucratic nostalgia. Hershey Noailles preserves an actual vector-display lineage.
- **Early digital / internet:** Hershey Noailles, Sector, Exposure, and Systm Hybrid Reverse each connect to a distinct technology: vector CRT drawing, LCD segmentation, phototypesetting exposure, and modular grids.
- **Display / atmospheric:** Lobular produces soft biological atmosphere through anatomy. Azabache is dark and ornamental without distressed effects. Canarina and Paysage provide broad, scale-conscious silhouettes. Pescante adds moving calligraphic pressure.
- **Vernacular:** Amakan’s sheet and stripe variants bring Filipino provenance and material logic. Choso contributes painted signage mass. Piscolabis feels like ordinary commercial ephemera rather than a design-school reenactment.
- **Wildcards:** Malebolge treats legibility as conflict. Faustina combines Latin, Cyrillic, Arabic, and Berlin-specific emoji. 29LT Idris pairs Arabic Naskh logic with Latin serif and sans companions.

## Variable and OpenType behavior

- Tanklager’s inspected source defines a `wdth` axis from 80 to 100.
- Exposure documents an exposure design range from −100 to +100; its actual OpenType tag remains blank until a licensed binary can be inspected.
- Bertin combines six information-design transformations across dot and square constructions; exact tags remain unrecorded rather than guessed.
- 29LT Idris exposes weight, contrast, and extension controls and extensive Arabic/Latin OpenType behavior. Exact axis tags await a licensed package.
- Roubaix Industrielle combines optical-size cuts, trap intensities, and variable files; the lab currently loads its licensed 8–13 point WOFF2 cut.

## Licensing problems

- The four supplied sites are discovery directories, not licensors. Their tags never replace the original foundry terms.
- Tunera’s retained packages each include an OFL file. ESA Le 75 also labels many families OFL, but the inspected font-only archives omit the license text, so those binaries remain outside the repository.
- Camelot trials are limited to testing and presentation and may not be publicly presented. Domicile test fonts are evaluation-limited. Neither is bundled.
- Atypical’s exact public license scope was not sufficiently clear, so Topos and Panos remain paid references pending checkout review.
- 3type advertises a Faustina Lite download, but its exact package license has not been verified; it remains reference-only.
- Paid families from 29LT, 205TF, Grilli Type, Displaay, Dinamo, Typotheque, Blaze Type, and Pangram Pangram require the appropriate desktop, web, or app license.

## Purchase and deprioritization

Worth purchasing or formally trialing first: **GT Mechanik** for a complete technical system, **Eliza** for digital-literary work, **Chairman** for UI/editorial/mono continuity, **Exposure** for meaningful motion, and **29LT Idris** for Arabic/Latin projects.

Deprioritize without deleting: **Recorder** until its axes prove more distinctive than the lab’s other broad variable systems; **Rondeau** until it materially outperforms Nyght Serif or Messer for a specific project; **Louche** until a project needs its exact display temperament; **Panos** unless Greek support is relevant to the active brief.

## Systems worth testing

- **Lobular + Chairman Text + Tanklager:** organic display, quiet body, compact industrial labels.
- **Eliza + Panos + GT Mechanik:** digital literary serif, multilingual UI sans, electromechanical data family.
- **Nyght Serif + Roubaix Industrielle + Lelo Mono:** libre editorial, industrial navigation, paid technical mono.
- **Exposure + Chairman Semi-Mono + Hershey Noailles:** variable phototype display, machine-readable body, vector-system accents.
- **Amakan + 29LT Idris + Tanklager:** material vernacular display, serious Arabic/Latin publishing, neutral technical labeling.

No final family is selected. Reference-only cards are now visually withheld rather than rendered with a misleading fallback; follow their official links for specimens or add a properly licensed binary later.
